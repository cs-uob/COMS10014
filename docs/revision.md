## Mock Exam

  Below are two mock exams which mimics the style and format of the December exam.
  These are just a guide adapted from previous year's exams to get you familiar with the exam format - the worksheets are equally as important in your preparation.

  All questions are multiple choice, where each question has exactly 1 correct answer.
  Note that calculators are **NOT** permitted and that you are permitted to use one double sided A4 sheet of notes (printed or handwritten).
